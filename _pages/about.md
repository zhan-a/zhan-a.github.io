Research
======

**Covariance estimation.** I reproduced the Ledoit–Wolf (2004) shrinkage estimator from its formulas and validated it two independent ways: against an existing implementation, and against the paper's analytic benchmark, which separates a bug from Monte Carlo noise. I then extended it to Jagannathan and Ma (2003), recovering the covariance matrix implied by a long-only portfolio constraint at every monthly rebalance. The adjustment the constraint makes lines up closely with shrinkage toward the identity matrix and hardly at all with shrinkage toward constant correlation. The write-up is being finalised, and the repository becomes public once it is.

**Empirical market microstructure.** I study how price information moves between the Kazakhstan Stock Exchange and the foreign venues where the same companies are co-listed. The design uses a natural experiment already in the data: one pair trades with no session overlap, two others overlap by four hours, and the venue is held fixed across them.

Much of that work turned out to be negative results, and I'm writing it up as such: an audit of every candidate trading strategy, each reported with the defect that invalidated it, from opening prices that could not be traded in size to look-ahead between signal and fill. I think the audit trail is more useful than a surviving result would have been.

**Assistive technology.** I co-lead [**Kozha**](https://kozha-translate.com), an open-source pipeline that translates speech and text into 3D sign-language animation. It won **1st place** at the [RESNA Student Design Competition](https://www.resna.org/) in 2026.
