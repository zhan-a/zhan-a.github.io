I'm Zhan, a third-year undergraduate at the [Kazakh-British Technical University](https://kbtu.edu.kz/) in Almaty, reading Data Science & Business Analytics alongside a University of London degree in Economics, Management, Finance and the Social Sciences. My interests are in high-dimensional statistics and in what happens when statistical methods meet real, badly-behaved financial data.

Research
======

**Covariance estimation.** I'm working through a from-scratch reproduction of Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage estimation, validated against the analytic risk benchmark rather than against a reference implementation alone, and extending it toward the constraint-implied shrinkage that portfolio weight constraints impose implicitly.

**Empirical market microstructure.** I study how price information moves between the Kazakhstan Stock Exchange and the foreign venues where the same companies are co-listed. The design uses a natural experiment already present in the data: one pair trades with zero session overlap, two others overlap by four hours, and the venue is held fixed across them.

A good deal of that second project turned out to be negative results, and I've published them as such — a full-trial audit in which every candidate strategy is reported along with the defect that invalidated it, including a systematic screen of 253 parameter combinations where none survived multiple-testing correction. I think the audit trail is more useful than a surviving result would have been.

**Assistive technology.** I co-lead [**Kozha**](/portfolio/), an open-source sign language translator built for Kazakh and Russian Sign Language users, which won **1st place** at the [RESNA Student Design Competition](https://www.resna.org/) in 2026.
